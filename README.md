Overview
===

There is no bird in Minecraft ? Caelumpedion give you **BIRDS**

In version 0.1.0 :

- 10 family of birds (for ~50 species of bird)
- Entity fly & BOID system
- Bird feeder
- New color of feathers

You want to know what bird are in this mod ? Check the [Classification](https://github.com/Kalyptien/Caelumpedion-1.21.1/wiki/Classification) page for further information.

What next ?
===

A lot of content is missing from Caelumpedion :

- 20+ family
- More specific behavior
- Tame and Reproduction
- New mechanics
- Cosmetic
- And more...

So, keep on eye on Modrinth or Github !
